Our story

Started by dog lovers, for dog lovers.

Waggy Wonderland was founded by pet enthusiasts in London who wanted dog care that treats every dog as an individual. Walks, sits and sleepovers that cater to your dog's unique needs, not a queue.

Our Mission

To provide the most reliable, personalised, high-quality dog care, so every pet feels safe, comfortable and truly valued.

Our Commitment

Care that goes beyond the standard, from a team who keep learning. Built on safety, trust and one-on-one attention.
